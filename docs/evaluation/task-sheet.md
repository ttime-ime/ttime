# Task sheet

Open the Writing Room. Type in EWTS; Tibetan appears. Highlighted text is still
settling and becomes fixed when you press space or Enter. Think aloud if you can.

1. **Write a short phrase.** Type a line of Tibetan you would normally write, such as
   a title or a sentence from your work. Tell us when it looks right.
2. **Correct a mistake.** Change one syllable in what you wrote, in the middle of the
   line. Then add one more word at the end.
3. **Sanskrit.** Enter a Sanskrit mantra or a Sanskrit term from your own field,
   written in Tibetan script.
4. **Marks.** Add a mark you often need that is not an ordinary letter: a head mark,
   a special shad, a bracket, a number, or something else. Use whatever route you like.
5. **Inspect and copy.** Choose a sequence you find difficult. Look at what the page
   shows about it below the editor. Copy it.

Afterwards, if you are willing, open the **symbol review** page and look at a few
entries. Mark any label or EWTS that is wrong, and add anything missing.

Nothing is sent anywhere unless you press a button.
